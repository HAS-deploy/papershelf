Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. PaperShelf works without an account. Documents stay on your iPhone.
Is my paperwork uploaded?
No. Scanning and OCR run on-device. Nothing is sent to our servers.
What is free vs Unlimited?
Free includes scan, OCR, search, and export for up to 20 documents. Unlimited removes the cap ($19.99/year or $39.99 lifetime).

How much does Unlimited cost? $19.99/year or $39.99 lifetime. Free includes 20 documents.

How do I cancel? Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone.
